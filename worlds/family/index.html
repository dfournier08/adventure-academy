🌟 Example Super Challenge: Build a Birdhouse

Grandpa: help plan, measure, cut, assemble, and test.

Grandma: help choose a theme, decorate, weather-protect, and create a bird-watching journal.

Learner: research a local bird, set requirements, make design choices, track cost, choose placement, and observe whether birds visit.
