Grandpa's Workshop

Build It • Fix It • Invent It

This workshop turns Academy skills into real projects. The goal is not to follow every instruction perfectly — it is to plan, build, test, learn, change, and try again.

🔨

Build It

Start with requirements and create something that works.

🔧

Fix It

Observe a problem, diagnose it, try a fix, and retest.

💡

Invent It

Get a mission and constraints — then create your own solution.

Example mission: Build something that can move a ping-pong ball at least three feet without touching the ball after it starts.
