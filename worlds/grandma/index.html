Grandma's Creative Workshop

Craft It • Make It • Grow It • Cook It • Give It

Creative projects that mix imagination with real skills such as planning, patterns, measurement, following a process, making choices, and creating something for someone else.

🎨

Craft It

Painting, decorating, paper crafts, and seasonal creations.

🧵

Make It

Bracelets, simple sewing, fabric, patterns, and handmade gifts.

🍪

Cook It

Measure, follow steps, make predictions, and create something tasty together.

🌱

Grow It

Plant, care, observe, measure, and learn how living things change.

🎁

Give It

Create something for another person and think about what they would enjoy.

♻️

Reuse It

Turn ordinary materials into something useful, fun, or beautiful.

Example mission: Design a friendship bracelet using at least three colors and a repeating pattern. Measure first, draw your pattern, make it, then decide what you would improve next time.
